Platform

What We Build

A complete pipeline from raw taxi trips to deployable recommendation policies.

📊

Demand Forecasting

Leakage-safe LightGBM and XGBoost models with GraphSAGE/GAT spatial features. Strictly-prior temporal splits guarantee no future information leakage.

🏙️

Multi-Agent Simulation

Finite-demand simulator with configurable fleet size, explicit competition, and demand depletion. Tests policies under realistic market pressure.

🧠

Reinforcement Learning

Gymnasium-compatible environment with DQN and Double DQN baselines. Masked action spaces reflect real-world travel constraints.

📐

Reproducible Benchmark

All metrics checked into the repository. Paired statistical tests, horizon experiments, and exposure analysis with full audit trails.
